Costs & Savings

Understanding Solar Investment

Get a clear picture of costs, subsidies, financing, and the payback period.

How much does a solar system cost?

Costs vary based on system size, panel type, and installation complexity. A typical 3kW residential system in India costs between ₹1.8–2.5 lakhs before subsidies. After the 40% government subsidy, the net cost drops significantly.

  • 3kW system: ₹1.8–2.2 lakhs (after subsidy: ₹1.1–1.3 lakhs)
  • 5kW system: ₹3.0–3.8 lakhs (after subsidy: ₹1.8–2.3 lakhs)
  • 10kW system: ₹5.5–7.0 lakhs (after subsidy: ₹3.3–4.2 lakhs)
What is the payback period?

Most homeowners recover their investment within 4–6 years through electricity bill savings. With current tariffs and government subsidies, the average payback period in India is just 4.2 years. Over 25 years, you could save ₹2.3 lakhs or more.

What government subsidies are available?

The Indian government offers up to 40% subsidy on residential solar installations through the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE). SolarNova handles all the paperwork for you — we apply for the subsidy on your behalf, so you get the benefit without any hassle.

Can I finance my solar system?

Yes! We partner with leading banks to offer zero-down payment EMI options with interest rates starting at 7.5%. You can finance up to 100% of the system cost and pay back in easy monthly instalments — often less than your current electricity bill.

Installation & Maintenance

Everything you need to know about the installation process and keeping your system in top shape.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installations are completed within 3–7 days. Our certified team handles everything — from site assessment and mounting to wiring, inverter setup, and final testing.

What maintenance is required?

Solar systems require very little maintenance. We recommend cleaning panels with water every 3–6 months and an annual professional inspection. We offer annual maintenance plans starting at ₹3,500/year.

Storage & Metering

Battery Storage & Net Metering

Maximize your savings with energy storage and grid connectivity.

What is net metering and how does it work?

Net metering allows you to export excess solar energy to the grid and receive credits on your electricity bill. When your system produces more power than you consume, the meter runs backward — you're essentially selling energy to the utility.

Do I need battery storage?

Battery storage is optional but recommended if:

  • You experience frequent power outages
  • Your utility doesn't offer net metering
  • You want 100% energy independence
What happens during a power outage?

With a grid-tied system (no battery), your solar system will automatically shut off during a grid outage for safety reasons. If you have battery storage, you'll continue to power essential appliances during blackouts.

Impact

Environmental Impact & Future

See how going solar benefits the planet and what lies ahead for solar energy.

How much CO₂ does a solar system save?

A 5kW system saves about 3–4 tons of CO₂ per year — equivalent to planting 50–70 trees annually. Over 25 years, that's nearly 100 tons of CO₂ avoided.

Are solar panels recyclable?

Yes — up to 95% of a panel's materials can be recycled, including glass, aluminum, and semiconductor materials. We partner with certified recyclers for responsible disposal.

What's the future of solar in India?

India targets 280 GW of solar capacity by 2030. With costs dropping 80% over the last decade, now is the ideal time to go solar.

Can I add more panels later?

Yes — our systems are designed to be scalable. You can add more panels or battery capacity as your energy needs grow.
