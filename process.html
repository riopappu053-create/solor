FAQ

Common Questions About the Process

Quick answers to the most frequently asked questions about our solar installation process.

How long does the entire process take?
From consultation to activation, the entire process typically takes 7 to 10 days. This includes site assessment, design, approvals, installation, and commissioning. The timeline may vary slightly based on permit approvals and weather conditions.
Do I need to be home during installation?
You don't need to be present for the entire installation, but we recommend being available for the initial inspection and final walkthrough. Our team coordinates with you to schedule the installation at your convenience.
Who handles the subsidy paperwork?
We handle all subsidy paperwork on your behalf. Our team prepares and submits the application to MNRE and DISCOM, so you don't have to deal with any bureaucratic hassle. We'll keep you updated on the status.
What happens after installation?
After installation, we conduct a thorough inspection and commission your system. We then set up your smart monitoring app, provide a detailed handover, and schedule a follow-up visit. Our support team is available 24/7 for any questions.
Is there a warranty on the installation work?
Yes. We offer a 5-year installation warranty on all workmanship. This covers any issues related to installation quality, wiring, mounting, and structural integrity. The panels and inverters come with their own manufacturer warranties (25 years for panels, 5-10 years for inverters).
