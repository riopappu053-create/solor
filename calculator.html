Methodology

How We Calculate Your Savings

Our calculator uses real-world data and industry-standard formulas to give you accurate, reliable estimates.

Step 1: Energy Production

System Size (kW) × Sun Hours × 365 × 0.85 (system loss factor)

kWh/year = kW × hrs × 365 × 0.85

Example: 3.5kW × 5.5hrs × 365 × 0.85 = 5,980 kWh/year

Step 2: Cost Savings

Energy Produced × Current Electricity Tariff

Savings = kWh/year × ₹/kWh

Example: 5,980 kWh × ₹8 = ₹47,840/year

Step 3: Subsidy Adjustment

Government subsidy reduces upfront cost by up to 40%

Net Cost = Total Cost × (1 - Subsidy%)

Example: ₹2,10,000 × 0.60 = ₹1,26,000

Step 4: Payback Period

Net Cost ÷ Annual Savings

Payback = Net Cost / Annual Savings

Example: ₹1,26,000 / ₹47,840 = 2.6 years

All calculations are estimates based on average solar irradiance in India. Actual savings may vary.

Tips

Calculator Tips & FAQ

Get the most accurate estimates and answers to common questions about solar savings.

Check Your Bill Carefully

Use your average monthly bill over the last 12 months for the most accurate estimate. Include all charges like GST and service fees.

Adjust Sun Hours for Your Location

India receives 4-7 peak sun hours depending on region. Delhi/NCR: 5.5 hrs, Mumbai: 5.0 hrs, Bangalore: 5.5 hrs.

Include Subsidy in Your Calculation

Government subsidy significantly reduces payback period. Our calculator automatically applies the 40% subsidy for residential systems.

Factor in Tariff Escalation

Electricity tariffs increase 3-5% annually. Our calculator accounts for this, showing you even higher savings over time.
