Quick Questions

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive. For more details, visit our FAQ page.

Q.

How much does a solar system cost?

Costs vary by system size. A 3kW system starts at ₹1.8 lakhs before subsidy. Contact us for a custom quote.

Q.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes! We provide free, no-obligation consultations. We'll assess your roof, energy needs, and provide a transparent quote.

Q.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installations are completed in 3–7 days. Commercial systems may take longer depending on scale.

Q.

What warranty do you provide?

We offer 25-year performance warranty on panels, 5–10 years on inverters, and 5-year workmanship warranty.
